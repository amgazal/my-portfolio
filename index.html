About

I care about what the interface helps someone do.

Moving from Kumasi to Ithaca made me notice how often software can be technically correct without being useful enough. That idea eventually became Layer, and it still shapes how I approach frontend work.

01

Start with the decision

I like interfaces that make the next step obvious. Before adding another component or screen, I try to understand what the user is actually deciding or trying to finish.

02

Keep the engineering close

Frontend is the part I enjoy most, but I want to understand the data, backend, tests, and deployment behind the screen too. That makes me more useful when a problem crosses boundaries.
