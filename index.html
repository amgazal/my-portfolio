Layer

A personalized weather app that turns forecast data into clothing guidance and learns from user feedback.

The idea came from adapting to Ithaca weather after growing up in Kumasi. A forecast could be accurate and still leave me unsure what to wear. I built Layer to close that gap. What started as a frontend idea grew into persistence, sync, authentication, feedback, and testing.

AI4ALL · Team project 2026

AI Tweet Detection

An AI4ALL team project that compares three models for human vs. AI-generated tweet classification and makes the results explorable in Streamlit.

We merged TweepFake and ElectAI into a 35,443-tweet benchmark and evaluated BERT, Random Forest, and Logistic Regression on the same task. The most useful result was not just the headline accuracy; a per-source breakdown exposed topic leakage that made one model look stronger than it really was.

  • Cleaned and standardized 35K+ tweets while preserving source and generator labels for analysis.
  • Compared transformer and TF-IDF-based models using accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and source-level checks.
  • Helped package the work into a Streamlit app with predictions, confidence, and model comparison.

About

Good software should respect the person using it.

A lot of my work has put me directly in front of the person relying on the outcome: troubleshooting a laptop while someone waits, helping people through busy service shifts, teaching students who are stuck, and maintaining a site attendees depend on for event information. That has made me pay close attention to the small points of friction that decide whether software feels dependable or frustrating.

That changes how I build interfaces

I pay attention to clear states, useful feedback, sensible defaults, responsive behavior, and what happens when something goes wrong. A user should not have to understand the system underneath to feel confident using it.

Frontend first, not frontend only

The interface is where I am most at home, but I like knowing what sits behind it. Understanding APIs, data models, authentication, testing, and deployment helps me make better decisions at the screen and debug problems instead of simply passing them downstream.
